Refund Policy

Customers can request a refund within 7 days of receiving an eligible product.

The product should normally be unused and in resalable condition.

Refund requests are reviewed by the support team.

Once approved, refunds are normally processed within 5-7 business days.

Some digital products and services may not be eligible for refunds.

The AI agent must never claim that a refund has been approved unless the system explicitly confirms approval.
